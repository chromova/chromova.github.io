Delete Your Chromova Account

Request deletion of your account and all associated data.

Warning: Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Option 1: In the App

Open Chromova > Settings > Account > Delete Account > Confirm

Option 2: Email

Send to support@alegya.com with subject Account Deletion Request and your registered email. We respond within 7 business days.

Data Deleted

  • Email and username
  • Game progress, scores, coins
  • Leaderboard entries

Retention

Anonymized analytics retained up to 90 days. No personal data kept after deletion.
